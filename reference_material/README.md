# reference_material/

Samples of what you are trying to produce: a graded exemplar, a previous report of
your own, a departmental template, something your supervisor pointed at.

`/onboard` reads what is here and extracts a style profile — voice, tense, citation
density, how theory is integrated, how formal the register is. It is much faster than
answering questions about your own writing conventions, and more accurate.

A failed previous attempt belongs here too, and is just as useful. Onboarding asks
what to copy and what to avoid.
